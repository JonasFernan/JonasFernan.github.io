About Me

SEC. 03 — ABOUT

I'm an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Texas at Arlington with a hands-on focus on aircraft and UAV systems. I'm drawn to the full lifecycle of a build — from CAD and structural sizing through manufacturing, electronics integration, and flight or bench testing.

Outside of coursework, I work on independent projects that let me apply aerospace fundamentals to physical hardware: building and flying a UAV, designing and launching a high-power rocket, and developing autonomous and feedback-controlled systems from the ground up. I care about getting hardware to actually work, understanding why it fails when it doesn't, and iterating until it does.

I'm open to aerospace engineering internship opportunities where I can bring that same approach — design, build, test, iterate — to real hardware alongside an experienced engineering team.
